## What is published

Copilot bills chat, CLI, cloud agent, Spaces, Spark, third-party agents, and code review in GitHub AI Credits. **1 AI credit = $0.01 USD** of GitHub's own model cost. Code completions and next edit suggestions do not spend credits. Paid plans keep those unlimited. Free inline suggestions are 2,000 per month.

The allowance is one shared monthly pool, not a per-model quota. Base credits match the subscription price and are described as never changing. Flex is extra included usage and is described as variable. Unused credits do not roll over. The pool resets at 00:00:00 UTC on the first calendar day of the month.

| Plan | Price | Base | Flex | Total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Pro | $10 | 1,000 credits ($10) | 500 ($5) | 1,500 ($15) |
| Pro+ | $39 | 3,900 ($39) | 3,100 ($31) | 7,000 ($70) |
| Max | $100 | 10,000 ($100) | 10,000 ($100) | 20,000 ($200) |

Free shows "Limited chat and agent usage" and does not publish a credit count. Student is described as having some credits and unlimited completions; no Student credit count is on these pages. Business is $19 per user and Enterprise is $39 per user; on the plans page their flex row is disabled and the credit cells equal the per-user price. Individual plans are not the org-wide pooled row.

Paid plans get a 30% discount on model cost for Copilot Fusion through 2026-11-30, and a 10% discount on model cost for auto model selection in Copilot Chat and Copilot cloud agent. Gemini 3.8 Flash, Gemini 4 Argon, and Gemini 4 Carbon rows are labeled promotional.

## What this record does not do

It does not convert credits into tokens. It does not assert that a GitHub credit equals the underlying provider's list-API value. Three older rows already in this repo (Opus 5.5, Grok 4.7, Kimi K3) match the corresponding GitHub default numbers, and those archives are dated 2026-09-05 through 2026-09-23. Provider price lists were not re-fetched on 2026-11-16. The plans-page model matrix and the rate table are not the same set of models. The legacy annual request-based multiplier page was not fetched.
